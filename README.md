# R.A.S. Services: Main Site (Holding Page)

**Research · Academic · Support | Polokwane, Limpopo, South Africa**

This repository hosts the temporary holding page for [rasservices.co.za](https://rasservices.co.za) while the main website is being rebuilt. Visitors see a short notice and are automatically redirected to our portfolio site, where our current services and past work are available.

**Portfolio and current services:** [portfolio.rasservices.co.za](https://portfolio.rasservices.co.za)

---

## About R.A.S. Services

R.A.S. Services is a research and academic consultancy supporting undergraduate and postgraduate students across South Africa. Our current work includes:

- Research framework consulting and structural guidance
- Academic editing and submission-readiness reviews
- Data analysis (quantitative, qualitative and mixed methods)
- Consultation and academic mentorship
- Tertiary and bursary application support

Full details, pricing and case studies are on the [portfolio site](https://portfolio.rasservices.co.za).
